## 1. Executive Summary

A forensic automated audit was executed across all 20 demonstration scenarios registered in `demo_corpus/`. Every scene was evaluated against six mandatory physical Earth Observation criteria:
1. **Valid GeoTIFF Structure:** File opens cleanly via `rasterio` with non-empty band arrays.
2. **Standard Spatial Reference:** Verified projected CRS (UTM Zones 42N, 43N, 44N, 45N, 46N) and WGS84 geographic bounding coordinates.
3. **Calibrated Ground Sample Distance:** Exact $10.0\text{m}$ spatial pixel resolution.
4. **Dimensions & Channels:** Minimum $256 \times 256$ pixels (standard $512 \times 512$), 4-band optical (Blue, Green, Red, NIR) or 2-band radar (VV, VH).
5. **Cryptographic Integrity:** Local file byte stream exactly matches the SHA-256 digest in `manifest.json`.
6. **Preview Co-Registration:** Non-empty True-Color RGB or calibrated SAR grayscale preview PNG.
